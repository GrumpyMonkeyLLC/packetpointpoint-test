Our Distinctives

What sets Buckeye Threads apart in the fastener industry

Over 42 Years of Excellence

Since 1984, Buckeye Threads has been serving the industrial fastener needs of manufacturers, contractors, and businesses throughout Ohio and beyond. Our longevity in this competitive industry speaks to our commitment to quality, service, and customer satisfaction.

Veteran-Owned Family Business

As a proud veteran-owned family business, we bring the values of integrity, discipline, and service to everything we do. Our family atmosphere extends to our customers, creating long-lasting relationships built on trust and mutual respect.

ISO 9001:2000 Quality Standards

We maintain a quality manual that conforms to ISO 9001:2000 standards, ensuring consistent quality in every product we deliver. Our commitment to quality management means you can rely on us for precision and reliability.

Next Day Delivery

We understand that downtime costs money. That's why we offer next day delivery on hard-to-find sizes and maintain extensive inventory to keep your projects moving forward without delay.

Custom Manufacturing Capabilities

Whether you need 100 pieces or 100,000, we can manufacture specials to your exact specifications. Our domestic and overseas partnerships allow us to handle projects of any scale while maintaining quality standards.

Comprehensive Product Range

From standard fasteners to exotic alloys, from cutting tools to custom stampings, we're your one-stop shop for threaded products and industrial supplies. Our extensive product line means fewer vendors and simpler procurement for you.

Personalized Service

Unlike larger distributors, we provide personalized attention to every customer. When you call Buckeye Threads, you'll talk to knowledgeable staff who understand your needs and can provide solutions, not just take orders.
