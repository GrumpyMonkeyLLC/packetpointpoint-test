Surplus & Overstock Items

Buckeye Threads regularly has surplus and overstock inventory available at discounted prices. These are quality fasteners from reputable manufacturers that we can offer at substantial savings.

What We Offer

Our surplus inventory may include:

  • Overrun production quantities
  • Discontinued items in excellent condition
  • End-of-lot materials
  • Customer returns in original packaging
  • Warehouse clearance items
  • Special purchase opportunities

Quality Guarantee

All surplus items meet the same quality standards as our regular inventory. Just because they're priced lower doesn't mean they're lower quality. These are genuine, first-quality fasteners that simply need to be moved to make room for new inventory.
