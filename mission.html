Our Mission

Committed to quality, service, and customer success

Mission Statement

At Buckeye Threads, our mission is to be the trusted partner for all fastener and industrial supply needs by providing:

  • Quality Products: We supply only fasteners and tools that meet or exceed industry standards, ensuring reliability in every application.
  • Exceptional Service: We treat every customer with respect and provide personalized attention to understand and meet their unique requirements.
  • Technical Expertise: Our knowledgeable staff provides guidance on product selection, specifications, and applications.
  • Reliable Delivery: We maintain extensive inventory and offer next-day delivery on hard-to-find items to keep your projects on schedule.
  • Custom Solutions: We manufacture specials to your exact specifications, providing flexibility for unique applications.
  • Fair Pricing: We offer competitive pricing while maintaining the quality and service you deserve.

Our Values

As a veteran-owned family business, we operate according to core values that guide everything we do:

  • Integrity: We conduct business honestly and transparently, building trust through ethical practices.
  • Excellence: We strive for excellence in product quality, customer service, and business operations.
  • Customer Focus: Our customers' success is our success. We're committed to understanding and exceeding their expectations.
  • Continuous Improvement: We continuously seek ways to improve our products, processes, and services.
  • Community: We're proud to serve our local community and contribute to the success of businesses in our region.

Our Promise

"Customers are the Nuts and Bolts of our Business" isn't just a tagline—it's a promise. We're committed to being your reliable partner for fasteners and industrial supplies, providing the products and service you need to succeed.

Looking to the Future

While we honor our 42-year heritage, we're also looking forward to continued growth and innovation. We're committed to staying current with industry developments, expanding our capabilities, and continuing to serve our customers with the same dedication that has defined us since 1984.
