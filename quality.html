Quality Assurance

Our commitment to excellence in every fastener

ISO 9001:2000 Certification

Buckeye Threads maintains a comprehensive quality manual conforming to ISO 9001:2000 standards. This internationally recognized quality management system ensures that every product meets rigorous quality criteria and that our processes are consistently followed and continuously improved.

Quality Control Procedures

Our quality assurance program includes:

  • Incoming material inspection and verification
  • In-process quality checks at critical control points
  • Final product inspection before shipment
  • Dimensional verification using calibrated equipment
  • Material certification and traceability
  • Documentation and record keeping for full traceability

Material Standards Compliance

We ensure all fasteners meet applicable industry standards including:

  • ASTM (American Society for Testing and Materials) specifications
  • SAE (Society of Automotive Engineers) standards
  • ASME (American Society of Mechanical Engineers) requirements
  • IFI (Industrial Fasteners Institute) standards
  • ISO (International Organization for Standardization) specifications

Testing and Verification

When required, we provide comprehensive testing documentation including:

  • Material test reports and certifications
  • Hardness testing results
  • Tensile strength verification
  • Chemical composition analysis
  • Dimensional inspection reports
  • Thread verification and gauging

Supplier Quality Management

We carefully select and monitor our suppliers to ensure they meet our quality standards. Our supplier quality program includes regular audits and performance reviews to maintain the highest standards throughout our supply chain.

Customer Satisfaction

Quality isn't just about the product—it's about the entire customer experience. We measure our success by your satisfaction and continuously work to improve our service, delivery times, and product quality based on customer feedback.

Continuous Improvement

Our ISO-based quality management system emphasizes continuous improvement. We regularly review our processes, track quality metrics, and implement improvements to better serve our customers and maintain our position as a trusted fastener supplier.
