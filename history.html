Our History

Over four decades of service and excellence

Founded in 1984

Buckeye Threads, Inc. was founded in 1984 as a family-owned fastener distribution business in Norton, Ohio. From the beginning, our mission has been to provide quality fasteners and exceptional service to manufacturers, contractors, and businesses throughout the region.

Veteran-Owned Values

As a veteran-owned business, we've always operated with the principles of integrity, discipline, and commitment to service. These military values have shaped our company culture and our approach to customer relationships.

Growth and Expansion

Over the years, we've expanded our product line from basic fasteners to include specialty items, custom manufacturing capabilities, cutting tools, and power tools. Our growth has been driven by our commitment to meeting our customers' evolving needs.

Quality Commitment

In our pursuit of excellence, we implemented ISO 9001:2000 quality management standards, ensuring that every product meets rigorous quality criteria. This commitment to quality has been a cornerstone of our reputation in the industry.

42 Years and Counting

Today, Buckeye Threads continues to serve customers across Ohio and beyond. We've maintained our family business atmosphere while embracing modern technology and manufacturing capabilities. Our longevity is a testament to the trust our customers place in us and our dedication to their success.

Industry Involvement

We're proud members of STAFDA (Specialty Tools & Fasteners Distributors Association) and NFIB (National Federation of Independent Business), staying connected with industry trends and best practices while supporting the independent business community.

Looking Forward

As we continue into the future, we remain committed to the principles that have guided us for over four decades: quality products, fair pricing, exceptional service, and treating every customer with respect. We look forward to serving you and being your trusted fastener supplier for many years to come.
