Employment Opportunities

Join our team at Buckeye Threads

Work With Us

Buckeye Threads is a family-owned business that values hard work, integrity, and dedication. We're always interested in meeting talented individuals who share our commitment to customer service and quality.

Why Join Buckeye Threads?

  • Family-owned business with a supportive work environment
  • Veteran-owned company with strong values
  • Over 42 years of stability and growth
  • Opportunity to learn the fastener and industrial supply business
  • Competitive compensation
  • Opportunities for advancement

Positions We Hire For

Typical positions at Buckeye Threads include:

  • Sales Representatives: Customer-facing roles requiring product knowledge and customer service skills
  • Warehouse Staff: Order picking, packing, shipping, and inventory management
  • Customer Service: Phone and email support, order entry, and customer assistance
  • Technical Support: Product specification assistance and technical guidance

What We Look For

Ideal candidates demonstrate:

  • Strong work ethic and reliability
  • Attention to detail and accuracy
  • Customer service orientation
  • Ability to work in a team environment
  • Willingness to learn about fasteners and industrial products
  • Good communication skills
  • Basic computer skills

Veterans Encouraged to Apply

As a veteran-owned business, we strongly encourage veterans to apply. We understand and appreciate the skills and values that military service develops.
